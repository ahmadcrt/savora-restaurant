Our Story

Savora began with a simple belief: a meal is the best excuse to slow down. Our kitchen cooks from a short, seasonal list, with fire, butter and patience doing most of the work.

The room is warm and unhurried — low light, linen, burgundy leather — built for long conversations and second helpings of dessert.
